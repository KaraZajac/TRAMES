==============================================================================
CONE RADIUS SENSITIVITY  (paper section 7.3)
==============================================================================

17580 routes re-scored at radii [30, 45, 60, 90] m.
Routes were PLANNED against 60 m cones; only exposure is recomputed.

--- 1. EXPOSURE on the unavoided route ---
   radius     mean  median   >=1 cam   >=5 cam   vs 60 m
     30 m     4.21     2.0     74.6%     31.8%     0.75x
     45 m     5.09     3.0     77.4%     37.1%     0.90x
     60 m     5.62     3.0     78.7%     39.7% --
     90 m     6.29     4.0     80.3%     42.6%     1.12x

--- 2. AVOIDANCE ROBUSTNESS ---
  Share of the SAME routes (planned at 60 m) that still cross no cone
  when scored at each radius:
   radius     clean  mean exp   residual vs baseline
     30 m     85.5%      0.23                   5.6%
     45 m     83.6%      0.28                   5.4%
     60 m     81.6%      0.31                   5.6%
     90 m     52.4%      1.32                  21.0%

--- 3. DOES THE HEADLINE SURVIVE? ---
  at the assumed 60 m:      81.6% of commutes reach zero exposure
  at 90 m (50% wider):    52.4%
  absolute change:          -29.2 points
  95% CI on the change:     [-29.9, -28.6] points
  at 30 m (half):        85.5%

  Interpretation: routes planned against 60 m cones are re-scored here without
  re-planning. A router given wider cones would find different clean routes, so
  the figures above are a LOWER bound on what avoidance could achieve at those
  radii, not an estimate of it.
